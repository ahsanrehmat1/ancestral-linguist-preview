A few things you might wonder.

What will I find in my report?+

Your report brings together the family information you share with historical context, surname research, and language insights. It distinguishes documented details from possibilities that need further research.

Do I need a DNA test?+

No. This report explores family history through the names, places, stories, and records you provide. It does not provide genetic testing or infer ancestry from facial appearance.

What if I know very little about my family?+

A family name or a place can be a starting point. The depth of a report depends on the information and sources available; uncertain connections are clearly identified.
